About Me

I am currently a Research Scientist on the Apple Foundation Model (AFM) team. My current focus is on LLM post-training, especially agentic capabilities. My work also spans synthetic data generation, diffusion language models, and multimodal learning. I received my Ph.D. from the University of Hong Kong (HKU), where I was advised by Prof. Stephen M.S. Lee and Prof. Lingpeng Kong. During the first year of my Ph.D. studies and my Master's studies in HKU, I was fortunate to be supervised by Prof. Yu Leung Ho Philip.
